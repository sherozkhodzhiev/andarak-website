# Andarak — A History in Fire

## Original problem statement
Rebuild and upgrade a single-file HTML scrollytelling piece ("Andarak — A History in Fire": 10 chapters, 2,500 years of history of a Tajik-Persian village in the Fergana valley) into the React app in this workspace, with AI-generated historic artwork per chapter, per-scene ambient audio with smooth crossfade, and an EN/RU language toggle. User additionally required dynamic historical visual scenes for each era (warriors in battle, Persian/Sogdian figures, village life, authentic architecture). Quality bar: award-worthy motion design (framer-motion reveals, lenis smooth scrolling, kinetic hero, marquee, parallax).

## Architecture
- React SPA (no backend data needed; backend left as default health endpoint)
- `/app/frontend/src/data/chapters.js` — all 10 chapters, EN/RU copy, flame states, UI strings
- `/app/frontend/src/components/` — Hero (kinetic masked-line reveal + mouse parallax), Chapter (sticky art frame, ken-burns, embers), Marquee, Chrome (flame indicator, controls, dot nav, score cue)
- `/app/frontend/src/hooks/useAmbience.js` — procedural WebAudio soundscape engine (fire/storm/wind/calm/birds) with per-chapter crossfade; replaces the dead external audio URLs from the original file
- `/app/frontend/public/art/*.png` — AI-generated chapter paintings (Gemini image model via Emergent universal key), generated by `/app/scripts/generate_art.py`
- lenis for momentum scrolling, framer-motion for reveals

## User personas
- The author (from Andarak) presenting their village's history
- Russian- and English-speaking viewers (toggle)

## Implemented (2026-08-25)
- Kinetic hero with masked line-by-line reveal, parallax ghost "ANDARAK" layer, ember glow
- 10 numbered chapters with era-tinted atmospheres, scroll-reveal motion, sticky exhibition-style art frames with ken-burns
- 8 of 10 AI paintings generated and integrated (01–08)
- Procedural per-chapter ambient audio engine with crossfade; audio toggle (off by default, no autoplay)
- Full EN/RU language toggle across all copy
- Persistent flame indicator whose color/flicker state changes per era; chapter dot nav; "play through" auto-scroll mode (stops on manual scroll)
- Slow editorial marquee between hero and chapters; film grain + vignette; footer
